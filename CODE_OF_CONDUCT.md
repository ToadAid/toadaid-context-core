# ToadAid Community Code of Conduct

ToadAid welcomes people who want to build, test, document, review, or learn from this project.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- treat other people with respect;
- discuss technical disagreements on their merits;
- give useful, specific feedback without personal attacks;
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted abuse;
- keep security disclosures out of public threads when private reporting is appropriate.

## Project spaces

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, review threads, and other spaces where someone is representing ToadAid.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when conduct materially harms contributors or the project.

For conduct concerns that should not be public, contact `ToadaidDAO@gmail.com`.

Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome. Harassment is not.
